Method

01

Dynamics

The circular restricted three-body problem in the rotating frame, integrated with an eighth order Runge-Kutta method at a tolerance of 1e-12. The Jacobi constant checks every propagation.

02

Periodic orbits

Differential correction with the state transition matrix, then continuation from one converged orbit to the next. Stability and manifolds come from the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the monodromy matrix.

03

Observation

Sun and Moon from the JPL DE440 ephemeris, Earth rotation from IAU 2006 sidereal time, and access constraints that decide when a telescope can take a measurement.

These pages are precomputed, so the inputs are fixed. The full tool runs locally with an editable scenario, parameter sweeps, orbit determination filters and a GMAT export.
